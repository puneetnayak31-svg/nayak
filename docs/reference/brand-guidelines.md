# HIPL Brand Guidelines v1.0: Direction A, Sabha

**HIPL · Hinduism in Public Life.** *Heritage that thinks forward.* Many voices · One circle · Always moving.

## 01: The idea. Many voices. One circle.
The mark is a *sabha*, an assembly, drawn as a circle of voices. Every bar is a different person: a different length, a different opinion. Together they become a sun, the light of shared inquiry. It moves, it pulses, it never stands still, just like a living tradition.
- **The circle:** the sabha, the assembly where ideas are debated in the open.
- **The bars:** voices of every age (the elder, the student, the sceptic), each a different length.
- **The bindu:** the still centre, the question every conversation keeps returning to.

## 02: Logo system. One family, every size.
Primary lockup (horizontal) · Stacked lockup · Symbol (one colour) · Wordmark only (HIPL) · Mono dark (print, stamps, newspaper) · Mono light (over photos and video) · Social avatar / app icon / favicon.

## 03: Construction and alignment. One block, not two lines.
HIPL and its full name are built as a single justified block: same left edge, same right edge, joined by a saffron rule. The symbol is exactly as tall as that block. Always use the master artwork; never retype the logo.
- ✓ Justified block: edges line up, and the rule ties the two lines together. ✕ Ragged: the full name stops short and floats. ✕ Overhang: the full name runs past HIPL.
- **Justify.** HIPL and the full name share exactly the same left and right edges.
- **Bridge.** A saffron rule sits between the lines, with equal space (a) above and below it.
- **Match heights.** The symbol is as tall as the whole text block and centred on it.
- **Gap.** The space between symbol and text equals one bindu (the centre circle's width).
- **Capitals.** HIPL is always in capitals; the English full name is always in tracked capitals.
- **Small sizes.** Under 240 px wide, drop the full name and use symbol + HIPL.
- **Tagline apart.** The tagline is not part of the logo. It sits one bindu below, starts on the text's left edge and is never wider than the logo.
- **Master files only.** Never rebuild the logo from typed text; export from the master SVG.
- **Hindi lockup** (सार्वजनिक जीवन में हिंदू धर्म): words spread to HIPL's width. Never stretch letters; it breaks conjuncts.

## 04: Clear space and size
Keep empty space around the logo equal to **the diameter of the bindu × 1.5** on every side. Nothing (text, edges, other logos) enters that zone.
- Symbol minimum **24 px** (8 mm in print) · horizontal lockup minimum **96 px** · **below 240 px** wide, use symbol + HIPL only.

## 05: Colour. Night sky, festival light.
| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight | #14123A (RGB 20 18 58) | Primary, 40% |
| Saffron Flare | #FF8A1F (RGB 255 138 31) | Accent, 20% |
| Marigold | #FFC93C | Highlight, 10% |
| Lotus | #FF6FA3 | Pop, 5% |
| Chalk | #F7F3EC (RGB 247 243 236) | Ground, 25% |

Midnight carries the brand; saffron is the signature. Marigold and Lotus are sparks: use them for highlights, stickers and data, never for body text. Text on Chalk is always Midnight.

## 06: Typography. Loud headlines. Calm reading.
- **Display: Unbounded.** Headlines, posters, video titles. Short and punchy. The wordmark is always in capitals.
- **Text: Instrument Sans.** Articles, the Dispatch, captions, website. Clear and warm at every size.
- **Hindi: Mukta.** प्रश्न पूछना भी परंपरा है। ("Asking questions is also a tradition.")
- Scale: **H1 64/68, weight 800** ("Can tradition evolve?") · **H2 32/38, 600** ("One question. Two generations.") · **Body 18/29, 400** · **Label 13, 600** ("The Weekly Dispatch · Issue 01").

## 08: Please don't. Protect the circle.
✕ Don't stretch or squash · ✕ Don't use colours outside the palette · ✕ Don't add shadows, glows or bevels · ✕ Don't swap the font or lose contrast.

## 09: Voice. The smartest, warmest person at the chai stall.
- **We sound:** curious · confident · warm · witty · evidence-led · open to disagreement. "What would you keep, and what would you rethink?"
- **We never sound:** preachy · defensive · outraged · jargon-heavy · like a political campaign · holier-than-thou. "Only true believers will understand this."
