## The four looks
1. **Symbol + wordmark:** a teacup whose steam curls into a crescent moon
2. **Lettermark:** an "m" drawn as two sleeping eyes, with lashes and a little moon
3. **Emblem:** a night sky with a crescent and stars, ringed by rotating "Steep · Sip · Sleep"
4. **Editorial serif:** "mello" with a full moon for an "o" and z's floating up, over "SLEEP TEA"
