# Nayak Residence, Khurai: 3D interior concept

An interactive 3D model of the renovation, built from Shape Design Studio's
*Proposed Civil Layout* (07 Nov 2026). It uses the **Ground Floor Proposed Plan**
and the **First Floor Proposed Plan**. "Ground Floor Proposed Plan 2" is not used.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It needs internet access to load three.js.

## What it does
- **Outside / Ground floor / First floor** views. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and tap a room.
- **Walk inside**: first-person walk from the road, through the gate and up the stairs (arrow keys/WASD, or the on-screen pad on a phone).
- **Play tour**: a guided camera tour with captions.
- **Hall–kitchen separator** with three options: a wooden jaali, a half wall with fluted glass, or an open shelf.
- **Cut-away walls** and **evening lighting**.
- **Interior budget** for each room (mid-range local prices), with a split between "do now" and "can wait".

## Assumptions
- The unlabelled front area on the ground floor is car parking. On the first floor it is an open terrace.
- Floor-to-floor height is 10'-6". Side walls are shared with the neighbours.
- Prices are estimates, not quotes. Civil work, wiring, paint and flooring are excluded.
