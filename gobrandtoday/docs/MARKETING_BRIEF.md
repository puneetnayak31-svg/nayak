## 1. One-liner and elevator pitch

domain, social handles, score, logo, guidelines and launch content, all in one tab.

**Site headline:** “Your idea deserves a brand✦”

**Site sub-headline:** “Tell us what you’re building. We’ll find the name, domain, social handles and identity to launch it.”

**Elevator pitch (about 30 seconds):**
Naming and branding a new business usually takes nine browser tabs and three weekends. You try a thesaurus,
check GoDaddy, check Instagram, open Canva, hunt for fonts and colours, and keep notes on your phone.
GoBrandToday does all of it in one place. Describe your idea in a sentence, and it suggests names,
honestly checks whether the domains and social handles are free, scores each name out of 10 with a
transparent breakdown, and then builds a full brand identity: logo, colours, fonts, a brand book with
mockups, and launch content. It is made in India, priced in rupees, and free to start.

**Category:** AI brand studio (AI naming + availability checking + brand identity + launch kit).

**Eyebrow / tag line on the site:** “AI brand studio · Made in India”

## 2. The problem and the promise

**Problem (site copy):** “Stop searching. Start branding.” Founders juggle about nine tabs across three
weekends: a thesaurus, a domain registrar, a hosting site, instagram.com/…, x.com/…, a design tool,
Google Fonts, a colour-palette site and a notes app.

**Promise:** One tab. From idea to identity in minutes.

**Why it is different (positioning pillars):**

1. **All in one flow.** Name → domain → handles → score → identity → launch. No copy-pasting between tools.
2. **Honest availability.** It never pretends a domain or handle is free. Every result says how it was
   checked (see section 5).
3. **Transparent scoring.** The GoBrand Score shows *why* a name scores what it scores, component by component.
4. **Real design, not clip-art.** Logos are crisp vector (SVG) constructions with real typography, not blurry
   AI images. You get four genuinely different “looks” to choose from.
5. **India-first, world-ready.** ₹ pricing, .in domains, Indian-language name roots, a Devanagari-inspired logo
   style and meaning checks across languages. Switch to $ any time.
6. **Free to start, fair when you grow.**

## 4. How it works: the six steps (site copy: “From idea to identity in minutes.”)

1. **Describe your idea.** “One sentence is enough. ‘A cosy candle brand for Gen Z.’”
2. **Discover names.** AI suggests names in the style you choose, each with meaning and reasons.
3. **Check domains & handles.** Domains with prices, plus 10 social platforms.
4. **Choose your brand.** Shortlist, compare side by side and read the GoBrand Score.
5. **Generate your identity.** Pick one of four looks; get logo, colours, fonts and a full brand book.
6. **Launch.** “Bios, launch posts, website copy and a month of content ideas.”

Guest-first: you can start without signing up. Creating an account (email or Google) keeps your work.

### 5.3 GoBrand Score (out of 10)
A transparent score built from eight weighted parts. Every part is explained.

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Brandability | 18 |
| Memorability | 15 |
| Pronunciation | 13 |
| Domain availability | 13 |
| Distinctiveness | 12 |
| SEO potential | 11 |
| Global usability | 9 |
| Social availability | 9 |

- The score is marked **provisional** until domain and handle checks finish.
- **Risk flags:** checks the name for unfortunate meanings in several languages (including Hindi/Hinglish, Spanish, French,
  German, Portuguese, Italian and Arabic) and for being too close to famous brands.
- Site copy: “It’s guidance, not a guarantee.”

### 5.4 Honest domain checks with prices
- Endings checked: .com, .in, .ai, .io, .co, .app, .xyz, .co.in, .store, .studio.
- Checks go to the official registries (RDAP), and can be confirmed with a registrar (Name.com, Porkbun,
  GoDaddy, Namecheap or Hostinger, depending on setup).
- **Clear, honest labels:**
  - **Available**: a registrar confirmed it, with the real price.
  - **Likely free**: the registry has no record, but a registrar hasn’t confirmed it yet.
  - **Taken**.
  - **Not checked**: it couldn’t be checked, so you get a one-tap link to check it yourself.
- Prices show per year. When there is no live price, an estimate is shown and labelled **“est.”**
- **Buy links** go straight to registrars, including Indian storefronts.

### 5.5 Social handle checks on 10 platforms
- Platforms: Instagram, X, YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, Threads, Pinterest, Reddit, GitHub.
- **Checked automatically:** GitHub, Reddit, YouTube (where the platform allows a compliant check).
- **One-tap check:** Instagram, X, TikTok and the others don’t allow automated checks, so you get a link that
  opens the exact profile URL. Site copy: “no scraping, no fake answers.”

### 5.6 The Core 5 tag
One glance tells you whether a name has the five things most brands need: **.com, Instagram, X, YouTube and
LinkedIn**. All availability is shown in one clean panel used everywhere in the app.

### 5.7 Four looks: generative logo identities
- Before building the guidelines you see **four genuinely different looks** (logo + palette + fonts), and you can ask for more looks and switch later.
- **10 logo constructions:** Spark full stop, Signet badge, Editorial serif, Sticker stack, Symbol + wordmark,

- Every logo is **vector (SVG)**, with real fonts, so it stays sharp from a favicon to a shop sign.

### 5.8 Brand in a Box / Brand Bible (the guidelines)
A studio-style brand book, generated for your brand:
- **Brand strategy:** story, positioning, mission, vision, audience and insights, personality, brand archetype,
  messaging (one-liner, short, long and an elevator pitch) and the brand essence (one promise and three values).
- **Logo system:** primary logo, icon, clear space, minimum size, use on different backgrounds, and misuse examples (“don’ts”).
- **Colour:** 5 colour roles with HEX, RGB and CMYK values, usage proportions and **contrast-checked (WCAG) pairings**.
- **Typography:** a Google Fonts trio (display, body, data) with a type scale.
- **Pattern** made from the brand’s shapes.
- **Imagery direction**, with an AI **moodboard** and **concept sketches** (generated with a free image model).
- **Voice and UI:** tone of voice, do’s and don’ts, sample UI.
- **Taglines:** 6–8 options.
- **9 mockups:** business cards, app icon, social post, website, T-shirt, tote bag, coffee cup, shop sign, packaging.
- **Other looks**, so you can switch direction at any time. Every change is versioned, so you can undo.

## 11. FAQ answers (use as-is)

- **Is the domain availability real?** Yes. We check the official registries and, where set up, a registrar
  confirms it with the real price. Every result says how it was checked. If we can’t check something, we say
  so and give you a one-tap link; we never guess “Available”.
- **Can you check Instagram, X and TikTok handles?** Where a platform offers a compliant way to check (GitHub,
  Reddit, YouTube) we verify automatically. Instagram, X, TikTok and others don’t, so we give you a one-tap link
  to confirm. No scraping, no fake answers.
- **What is the GoBrand Score?** A transparent 0–10 score combining brandability, memorability, pronunciation,
  distinctiveness, global usability, SEO potential, and domain and social availability. Every component is
  explained. It’s guidance, not a guarantee.
- **Do I own what I create?** Yes. Export your Brand Bible as PDF, PNG, SVG, JSON or Markdown. Fonts are free
  Google Fonts. Before you invest, run a proper trademark search; we flag obvious risks, but it isn’t legal clearance.
- **Is it built for India?** India-first: ₹ pricing, .in domains, Indian-language name roots, meaning checks
  and Indian registrar storefronts. Switch to $ any time.

## 13. Claims guardrails (must follow)

These keep the marketing truthful. The product is built around honesty, so the marketing must be too.

1. **Social checks:** never say Instagram, X, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, Threads or Pinterest handles are
   “verified” or “checked automatically”. Only GitHub, Reddit and YouTube are. For the rest, say “one-tap check”.
2. **Domains:** don’t say “guaranteed available” or “real-time availability for every domain”. Say “honestly
   checked” and “we tell you how each result was checked”. Prices without a live quote are estimates.
3. **No legal or SEO promises:** the score and risk flags are **not** trademark clearance, and SEO potential is
   **not** a ranking promise. Always pair names with “run a trademark search before you invest”.
4. **Payments are not live yet.** Prefer “Start free” and “Create My Brand” CTAs. Don’t run “Buy Pro now” or
   discount campaigns until payments launch. Show prices only as plan pricing.
5. **Studio:** don’t advertise “Priority checks” or “Brand approvals and comments” as available; they aren’t built
   yet. Team seats, domain and handle monitoring, and API access are “coming soon”.
6. **“Unlimited”** on Pro always means **unlimited (fair use)**.
7. **Logos are vector constructions plus AI-drawn symbols, not AI-generated images.** Image AI is used only for
   moodboards and concept sketches. Don’t claim “AI paints your logo”.
8. **Experts are fulfilled manually.** Requests are reviewed by the team. Confirm the expert network and capacity
   before advertising specific services, turnaround times or guarantees.
9. **Demo numbers are illustrative.** Kettlo, its score and its availability are example data. Label them
   “example” if shown, and don’t present them as a real customer.
10. **No invented social proof:** no fake testimonials, user counts, ratings, press logos or customer brands.
    Add these only when real ones exist.
11. **Trademarks:** don’t add ™ or ® to “GoBrand Score” or “GoBrandToday” unless the founder confirms it is filed.
12. **Competitors:** don’t name or mock competitor brands (GoDaddy, Canva, Looka and others) in ads. The “nine tabs”
    story describes generic tasks, not companies.
13. **“AI”**: the app has an offline fallback generator when AI isn’t available, and labels it. Say “AI-powered”,
    not “every result is written by AI”.
