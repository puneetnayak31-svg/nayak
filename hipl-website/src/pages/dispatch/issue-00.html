01One idea worth thinking about

Can a tradition question itself?

Some people think a tradition stays strong by never changing. Others think it stays alive only by arguing with itself. The classical schools of Indian philosophy spent centuries asking each other "how do you know that?" Maybe questioning isn't a threat to tradition. Maybe it is one of its oldest habits.
