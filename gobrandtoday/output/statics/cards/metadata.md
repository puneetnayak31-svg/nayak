# The GoBrand Collection: trading-card carousel

The five example brands from our videos, shown as collectible trading cards. Each card has a GoBrand Score, the eight score parts as stat bars, the one sentence it came from, the idea behind its logo and one honest watch-out. It reads like a game, but every element on a card is a real part of the product.

## Engagement ideas

- **Repeat as a series:** to add a card, make a new example brand in the app, then copy a card block in `statics/src/deck.js`.
