How a Dialogue works

About 90 minutes. Five moves.

  1. The room votesAgree, disagree or unsure, before anyone speaks.
  2. Each side makes its caseTwo or three speakers per side.
  3. Back-and-forthA moderator presses both sides on the strongest points.
  4. Your questionsFrom the floor and from the question box online.
  5. The room votes againWe announce the shift, not a winner.
