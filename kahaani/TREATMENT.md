# KAHAANI · "IPL की पहली रात": visual treatment

Re-edit of the Sports Tak *Kahaani* episode with Vikrant Gupta on how the IPL was born
(master: 11:33, 1920×1080, 25 fps). The picture is re-edited and the programme timing and audio stay exactly as they are,
so every sponsor pre-roll, L-band and end card stays where it was sold.

Status: **two sections are rendered as samples** (cold open 0:00–0:50 and the climax "The first night"
7:16–10:00). The rest of the film is planned below, beat by beat, and still needs sign-off.

## 2. Visual language

- **Anchor:** Vikrant stays the spine. About 60% of runtime is him full-frame. Treatments land on specific words and then get out of the way.
- **Palette:** ink (#090D0B), white, Sports Tak lime (#78D628) as the single accent, amber only for results.
- **Type:** Anton for numbers and English heads, Mukta ExtraBold for Hindi, JetBrains Mono for kickers and data. The Hindi always quotes or closely tracks his own words.
- **Archival grade** on photos: desaturated, slight warmth, grain and vignette. Every photo moves slowly; none is static.
- **Recurring devices (each has a job):**
  - **Depth title:** big type set *behind* Vikrant via a person matte. Kept for naming moments only: the date, ICL, IPL.
  - **Question stack and chapters:** his four questions from the open come back as chapter markers.
  - **Portrait mode:** when text needs the side of the frame, the room (and the burned-in logos) melts into a deep blur while he stays sharp.
  - **Split screen:** a live panel of Vikrant plus a card, for people and data he is explaining.
  - **Full-bleed:** a photo takes the screen for 4–9 s when the moment is the picture (McCullum, SRK, RR).
  - **India map** (official boundaries, Natural Earth India-POV edition): appears twice, for the 8 cities and then the icon players.
- **Restraint rules:** no treatment over sponsor or promo windows (`edl.py` checks this automatically). No AI image of a real, identifiable person or real event. Personal anecdotes ("I dropped Dada at the airport…") stay on Vikrant with only a slow push-in.

## 4. AI generation: where it earns its place

AI is used only for **atmosphere and metaphor, never for real people or real events**. Every AI shot carries an
on-screen `ILLUSTRATIVE RECREATION` tag (already built into the renderer). There are two shots in the whole film.

| # | Beat | Prompt (Runway / Veo / Kling, 16:9, 4 s, no text, no logos, no faces) |
|---|---|---|
| AI-1 | 1:56 "projects went to the dustbin" | *Close-up, a wire-mesh office wastepaper bin under a desk lamp at night, a thick stapled proposal crumpled on top, the word on the cover unreadable, shallow depth of field, slow push-in, 35 mm film grain, muted greens, 2000s office.* |
| AI-2 | 6:50 news blackout | *A wall of 2000s CRT television monitors in a dim newsroom control room, every screen showing static or black, one red tally light, slow lateral dolly, haze, documentary realism, no people, no readable text.* |

Rejected on purpose: AI "recreations" of the Delhi press event, the auction room or McCullum's innings. All three involve
real people, and real photos exist (or should be licensed).
