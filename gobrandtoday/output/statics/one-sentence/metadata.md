# One sentence: posts and an A3 print poster

These pieces skip the feature list. Each post shows one sentence typed into GoBrandToday and the brand that came out of it. The sentence ends with our spark instead of a full stop, so it reads like our wordmark. The last post leaves the sentence blank for the viewer to fill in.

## Poster: where to use it
- Co-working spaces, college E-cells, startup meetups, hackathons, café notice boards.
- Print on A3 matte (or A2: the PDF is vector and scales cleanly). Keep it uncoated if people will write on it.
- **Guerrilla idea:** leave a pen beside it with a sticky note that says "Write your sentence here ✦". Then photograph the notes for a Story.
