In 40 words

From the root dhṛ, to hold or support. Dharma is what holds things together: cosmic order, right conduct, duty and one's own path. Its meaning shifts with context, text and tradition.

A longer note

Dharma is one of the most important and least translatable words in Indian thought. In the Vedic period it is linked to , cosmic order. In later texts it covers ethics, law and social duty, and in the Bhagavad Gita it becomes personal as , one's own duty.

It is also one of the four aims of life, alongside artha, kāma and . Buddhist and Jain traditions use the same word with their own meanings.
