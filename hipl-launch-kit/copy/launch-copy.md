# HIPL launch copy

Ready-to-post text for launch week. Replace every `[BRACKET]` before posting. Never invent speakers, dates, quotes or facts; if something isn't confirmed, leave it out.

Voice check: curious, confident, warm, witty, evidence-led, open to disagreement. Never preachy, defensive, outraged, jargon-heavy or campaign-like.
