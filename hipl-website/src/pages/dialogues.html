How a Dialogue works

About 90 minutes. Five moves.

Dialogues follow the Oxford-style debate format, run with our debating partner, the Delhi Union. A motion is proposed, opposed, argued and put to the vote.

  1. The room votesFor, against or undecided on the motion, before anyone speaks.
  2. Each side makes its caseTwo or three speakers for the motion, two or three against.
  3. Back-and-forthA moderator presses both sides on the strongest points.
  4. Your questionsFrom the floor and from the question box online.
  5. The room votes againWe announce the shift, not a winner.
