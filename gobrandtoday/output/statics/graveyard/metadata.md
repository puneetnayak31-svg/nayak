# Brand graveyard: "RIP to the names that didn't make it"

A night-time graveyard of made-up brand names, each "born and died" sometime around 2 a.m. Each epitaph is a common naming mistake, and the "cause of death" is the GoBrand Score part that would have caught it. It's a meme that also teaches what the score checks.
