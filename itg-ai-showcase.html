Every project on this page follows the same recipe. An editor decides what the story is. AI speeds up the making: voices, visuals, translation, drafts. Then an editor signs off before anything reaches the audience.

Vice Chairperson Kalli Purie named it the AI Sandwich. The machine is the filling. Accountability stays with people.

    The stand we take

    A nine-point charter for AI and news

    At the India AI Impact Summit in February 2026, Kalli Purie set out nine points on fairness, accountability and reciprocity between AI companies and newsrooms. Three ideas run through it.

    Show how news is usedAI systems should be open about how they digest and reuse journalism.
    Pay fair valueReporting costs money to produce. AI cannot mine it for free.
    Own the mistakesHallucinations about news should carry real penalties, and humans stay accountable.
