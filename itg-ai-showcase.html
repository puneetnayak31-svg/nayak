The standard

A nine-point charter for AI and news

Presented at the India AI Impact Summit, New Delhi, February 2026.

  1. Fair value for journalism, and transparency on how AI uses news
  2. Traceability and attribution as a democratic principle
  3. Journalism recognised as a public good
  4. Rewards for stories that deliver social impact
  5. Real value for verified content from credible institutions
  6. Severe penalties for AI hallucinations
  7. No more asymmetry between legacy media and social platforms
  8. Citizens' attention treated as a rare, finite resource
  9. Reciprocity from the biggest tech companies
