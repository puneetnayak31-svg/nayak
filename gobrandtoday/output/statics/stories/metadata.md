# Stories: polls, an open call and a link story

Five 1080×1920 stories. Each one is built around an interactive sticker, because Stories that ask for a tap get more reach than Stories that only show something. Everything sits between y 250 and y 1580, clear of the app's top bar and reply bar. Each story also leaves a clean space for its sticker.

| File | Sticker to add | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `poll-1-mello-looks.png` | **Poll**: "A / B" (or "Editorial / Emblem") | the gap between the two panels (≈ y 940–1080) | Two of Mello's logo looks. Shows that every brand gets 4 looks. |
| `poll-2-loopa-looks.png` | **Poll**: "A / B" | same gap | Sticker stack vs Symbol + wordmark. Mentions 10 constructions and 13 symbol families. |
| `poll-3-names.png` | **Poll**: "Kettlo / Chaiwise" | same gap | Uses the product's own demo idea and names, labelled as examples. |
| `ask-drop-your-idea.png` | **Question**: "Your idea in one sentence" | where the arrow points (≈ y 960–1280) | Collects ideas from your audience to turn into follow-up posts. |
| `link-start-free.png` | **Link**: https://gobrandtoday.com, text "Start free" | on top of the violet pill (y 1290–1420) | The full flow at a glance: sentence → names → checks → brand. |

## Suggested sequence (one day)
1. `poll-3-names` in the morning (easiest to vote on).
2. `poll-1-mello-looks` around lunch.
3. `ask-drop-your-idea` in the evening.
4. `link-start-free` last, so it sits at the end of the tray.

Keep `poll-2-loopa-looks` for another day, or share it right after a Loopa post or Reel.

**Next day:** share the poll results with "You picked A ✦ Here's why it works", and post 2–3 of the ideas you collected as brands (feed post or Reel). If you use people's ideas, tag or credit them only with their permission.

## Guardrails respected
- Every brand, name and score is marked as an example (Mello, Loopa, Kettlo, Chaiwise, Sippet). Scores are the product's illustrative demo numbers.
- The link story's handle line uses the product's real labels: available, likely free, taken, one-tap check. It never claims a handle is "verified".
- The open call promises only to post a few brands. It doesn't promise to brand everyone or offer anything for free beyond the free plan.
